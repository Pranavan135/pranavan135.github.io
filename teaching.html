Teaching

Learning through Structure, Practice and Reflection

My teaching focuses on helping students build accurate mental models, connect theory with implementation and become thoughtful software engineers.

Teaching philosophy

Make Abstractions Observable

Students learn advanced programming concepts more effectively when the underlying structure is visible. I therefore combine explicit models, worked examples, carefully staged exercises and frequent feedback. My courses emphasise reasoning about program behaviour rather than memorising syntax.

  • ClarityExplain the purpose of an abstraction before introducing its mechanics.
  • PracticeMove from guided examples to independent implementation and design.
  • FeedbackUse labs, checkpoints and formative assessment to reveal misconceptions early.
