What is the algorithm doing?

K-means begins with randomly placed centroids. Every sample is assigned to its nearest centroid. Each centroid then moves to the mean position of the samples assigned to it. Repeating those two steps gradually stabilizes the clusters. No target label or answer key is provided — that is what makes this an unsupervised learning example.
